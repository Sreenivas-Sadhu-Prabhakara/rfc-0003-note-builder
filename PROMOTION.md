## Longer community post

How can an early network document help people today? Note Builder turns an idea from
RFC 3, “Documentation conventions,” into a small tool for community groups, students and people proposing their first project idea.
Compose a local discussion note with a preview, a small metadata checklist, question/suggestion/observation examples and a plain-text download. Keep uncertainty visible without imposing a polished proposal template. Inputs stay in the browser; you can download the result
and continue using the loaded page offline.

This is a local discussion format, not an official RFC template or submission service. Filling every field does not prove quality, agreement or authority. The kind selector and unreviewed banner are our additions. Blank context is advisory; a nonempty thought enables export. The official RFC status is UNKNOWN / Legacy, not HISTORIC;
this is not a current Internet standard. RFC 3 is obsoleted by RFC 10. RFC 10 is in turn listed as obsoleted by RFC 16. RFC 10 revises the note conventions and recipient list; RFC 16 adds an MIT recipient. These are historical records, not current contact or submission instructions.

## Small teaching exercise

Load the minimal example. Download the one-line observation with its context still blank. Add an author and a local reference: what becomes easier for a reader to discuss? Compare the two examples with the same title.

Expected observation: A thought without punctuation still exports. The downloaded text always labels itself as a local, unreviewed discussion note.
